    Module 1 to Module 3, including Module 1.1 - 1.9 , Module 2.1 - 2.5, and Module 3.1 - 3.4.

    • Curriculum

    This level of learning aims to provide families and healthcare professionals with foundational knowledge in functional nutrition to promote holistic wellness and support health management. Participants will gain an understanding of core nutrition principles, explore the role of functional foods and supplements, and learn about the digestive system’s impact on nutrient absorption and overall health. By mastering these concepts, learners will be equipped with basic nutritional knowledge to support individual and family health and address individual nutritional needs.
