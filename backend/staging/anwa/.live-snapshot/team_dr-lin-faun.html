    Dr. Lin Faun

    PhD level Clinical Nutritionist

    A dedicated Clinical Nutritionist with a PhD in Clinical Nutrition and over a decade of experience in personalized nutrition and clinical research. Passionate about integrating science with practical solutions.

    Dr. Lin Faun specializes in guiding individuals towards optimal health through evidence
    based nutrition and lifestyle strategies.

    With a commitment to education and empowerment, She helps clients and professionals alike navigate the complexities of health, bridging the gap between knowledge and real-life application.
